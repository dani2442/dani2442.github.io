I gave a talk at our internal machine learning seminar, together with Max Golightly, on the road that leads from optimal control to reinforcement learning.

We started from the deterministic picture — variational calculus, Hamilton–Jacobi, the Pontryagin maximum principle — and then moved to the probabilistic one, going through Markov decision processes, dynamic programming and policy gradient methods.

I’m sharing the slides here in case you’re interested in these topics [Link](/files/ml_seminar_optimal_control.pdf).
