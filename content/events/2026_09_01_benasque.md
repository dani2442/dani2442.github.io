I spent two weeks at the Benasque Science Center for the XI edition of “Partial Differential Equations, Optimization, Numerics, and Machine Learning” (August 23 – September 4, 2026), where I gave three talks and co-chaired the thematic session “PDEs and Machine Learning” together with Aidan Chaumet.

Here are the slides, in case you’re interested in these topics:

- **Hybrid Generative Modeling for Neuroscience** (August 25) — whole-brain dynamics and the fitting problem, joint work with L. Liverani, E. Zuazua and X. Kobeleva. [Link](/files/benasque_hybrid_generative_modeling.pdf)
- **Data-Driven Control in Infinite-Dimensional Spaces** (August 27) — informativity and persistency of excitation, with applications to data-driven controllability and LQR. [Link](/files/benasque_data_driven_control.pdf)
- **Neural ODEs: A Representer Theorem** (September 1) — joint work with Zhengping Ji. [Link](/files/benasque_neural_odes_representer_theorem.pdf)

The full program is available on the [conference website](https://benasque.org/2026pde/).
