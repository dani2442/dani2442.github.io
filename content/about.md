Hi, I'm Daniel López Montero. I'm a PhD student in mathematics at [Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU)](https://www.fau.eu/), in the [Chair for Dynamics, Control, Machine Learning and Numerics](https://dcn.nat.fau.eu/) (Alexander von Humboldt Professorship), supervised by [Prof. Enrique Zuazua](https://dcn.nat.fau.eu/enrique-zuazua/).

My research sits between machine learning and control theory: kernel methods and Gaussian processes, neural ODEs, physics-informed learning, data-driven control, and hybrid models of whole-brain dynamics. Before FAU I did an MSc in Mathematics and Applications at the Universidad Autónoma de Madrid (UAM) and worked at GMV on applied projects in energy systems, space operations and cybersecurity.

In winter semester 2026/27 I'm the teaching assistant for Prof. Zuazua's course *[Mathematical Foundations of Control and Machine Learning](https://dcn.nat.fau.eu/course-mathematical-foundations-of-control-and-machine-learning/)*.

On this site I post notes, projects and essays on topics I'm interested in. Talks and slides are in [events](/events/). Outside research I like to program, read, and play football, basketball and padel.
